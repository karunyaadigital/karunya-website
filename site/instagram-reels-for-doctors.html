Why doctors choose a healthcare-only social media team

A general agency writes for likes. Medical content has to be accurate, calm and within the rules. We write patient education, not promotion: no testimonials, no before/after photos, no superlative claims. Not comfortable on camera? Voiceover and motion-graphic Reels work too.

Every post is planned as patient education and checked against the NMC's Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication (October 2026). Nothing goes live without your approval.

Frequently asked questions

Do you send a camera crew to my clinic?

No. We write the scripts and guide your phone shoot (light, sound, framing), then handle editing, captions and posting.

Are Reels allowed for doctors under the NMC guidelines?

Educational content is allowed. What the October 2026 NMC guidelines restrict is promotion: testimonials, before/after content, 'best' or 'guaranteed' claims and solicitation. We plan every Reel as patient education and you approve it before it goes live.

I don't have time to shoot. What then?

Most doctors record a month's Reels in one short sitting. If you prefer not to appear, we make voiceover and motion-graphic videos.

Which languages do you write in?

Hindi, Hinglish and English, based on what your patients speak.

Do you also manage Facebook and YouTube?

Yes. The same content is adapted for Facebook and YouTube Shorts.
