FAQ

Questions doctors ask.

Do you follow the NMC guidelines?

Yes. Our content follows the NMC's Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication (October 2026): patient education first, no superlatives or guarantees, no testimonials, and no patient images. Every post goes live only after your approval.

How much of my time will this take?

One call to start. After that, approving the monthly calendar and a little time for shoots. Our team handles the rest.

How does the shoot work?

We give you the script and clear guidance on what to say, how to stand and which light to use. Editing, captions, posting and strategy are on us. We don't send camera crews; most doctors shoot on a phone.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at ₹20,000 per month, with Growth at ₹25,000 and Authority at ₹40,000. For full hospital marketing, call +91 73176 59337 for a custom plan.

What will I see every month?

A clear report with your reach, followers from your city, Google views, calls and direction requests, plus the plan for next month.
