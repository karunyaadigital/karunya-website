Digital marketing for doctors in India: a simple, NMC-safe plan to get found by patients

Bhanu Pratap Rai1 November 2026 · 7 min read

Your patients have changed. Before they visit, most of them search on Google, watch a few reels, read reviews and check your timings. Digital marketing for doctors simply means making sure that when they look, they find you, and they find accurate, helpful information.

This guide gives you a simple plan that works for clinics in cities like Varanasi, Meerut, Lucknow or Prayagraj, and that stays within the NMC's October 2026 guidelines.

First, understand how a patient actually finds a doctor

Think of the patient's journey in four steps:

Step What the patient does What you need
1. Worry Notices a symptom, asks family Helpful content they might come across
2. Search Types "gynaecologist near me" or a symptom in Hindi or English A complete Google Business Profile and a website
3. Check Looks at your profile, reels and reviews Educational reels, a clear bio, genuine reviews
4. Visit Calls, takes directions or messages on WhatsApp Correct phone number, timings and map pin

If any one step is weak, patients drop off. Good digital marketing fixes the whole path, not just one platform.

1. Google Business Profile: your most important free tool

When someone searches "doctor near me", Google shows the map listings first. That listing is your Google Business Profile.

  • Choose the correct primary category, for example "Gynaecologist" or "Orthopedic surgeon"
  • Add accurate timings, phone, WhatsApp and services
  • Upload real photos of your clinic, reception and team
  • Post a short health tip every week
  • Reply politely to every review, and never mention medical details

We have a full step-by-step Google Business Profile checklist for doctors.

2. SEO for doctors: answer the questions patients ask

SEO (search engine optimisation) means showing up when patients search a question. Patients rarely search your name. They search things like "periods late after stopping pills" or "knee pain while climbing stairs".

Simple ways to do it:

  • Have a website with a page for each service you offer
  • Write short articles answering the 10 questions patients ask you most
  • Use your city name naturally, for example "IVF treatment in Varanasi"
  • Make sure the site opens fast on mobile phones

3. Instagram and YouTube reels: let patients meet you first

Reels are where trust gets built before the first visit. A patient who has watched you explain their problem calmly already feels they know you.

What works well for doctors:

  • Myth vs fact reels about your speciality
  • "3 signs you should not ignore" style warning-sign reels
  • Answering one common patient question per reel
  • Explaining a procedure step by step, without promises

Keep a clear, factual bio: your name, qualifications, speciality, clinic and timings. Our guide on choosing a social media agency for doctors explains what to expect if you get help.

4. Your website: the home base

Social media platforms can change their rules overnight. Your website is yours. It should have:

  • Your qualifications and registration details
  • Services, timings, fees (if you choose to share them) and location
  • A WhatsApp or call button that works on mobile
  • Helpful articles that answer patient questions

5. What to avoid under the new NMC rules

Since 6 October 2026, the NMC's ethical advertising guidelines apply to all of this. Avoid:

  • Patient testimonials and before-and-after photos, even with consent
  • Words like "best", "No. 1", "guaranteed" or "100% success"
  • Buying followers, likes or reviews, or running review campaigns
  • Discounts, free consultation banners and offers
  • Ads that solicit patients for an individual doctor
  • AI avatars or AI-made patient stories

A realistic monthly plan

Every week Every month
2 to 3 educational reels Review what patients asked most, and plan next month's topics
1 Google Business Profile post Add new clinic photos
Reply to reviews and comments Check calls, direction requests and profile visits

Consistency matters more than going viral. A steady rhythm of useful posts builds trust far better than one lucky reel.

Doing it yourself vs working with a team

You can absolutely start alone with a phone and 20 minutes a week. As your practice grows, it helps to have a team handle research, scripts, editing, posting and reports, while you focus on patients and give a little time on camera. Personal branding works best as a team game between the doctor and the people behind the scenes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does digital marketing take to work for a clinic? Usually a few months of steady work. Google profile improvements can show early results; reels and SEO build up over time.

How much does digital marketing for doctors cost in India? It depends on the platforms and number of reels. At Karunya Digital, plans start at Rs 20,000 per month. See our pricing.

Is digital marketing allowed for doctors under NMC rules? Educational, accurate and non-promotional communication is allowed. Self-promotion, testimonials, guarantees and patient solicitation are not.
