About

From the ghats of Kashi, for doctors across India.

Karunya means compassion. Doctors practise it every day. Our job is to make sure it reaches the patients who need it.

A healthcare-only studio.

Karunya Digital is a healthcare marketing agency based in Varanasi. We work with 50+ doctors and hospitals across India, mostly in growing cities where there are many good doctors but few strong voices online.

Our team writes the scripts, guides the shoot, edits, designs and manages Google profiles, so doctors can give their full attention to patients.
