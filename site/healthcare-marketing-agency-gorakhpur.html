Why Gorakhpur doctors choose a healthcare-only agency

Most agencies work with every kind of business, from hotels to coaching centres. Medical marketing has its own rules. Since 6 October 2026, the NMC's Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication bar patient testimonials, before/after content, "best" or "guaranteed" claims, paid or solicited reviews and discount offers. We plan around these rules from the start, so your pages build trust without putting your registration at risk.
