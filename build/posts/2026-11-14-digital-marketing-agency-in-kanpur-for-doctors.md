## The NMC 2026 rules your agency must follow

The National Medical Commission's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* came into force on 6 October 2026. As reported by The South First, using third-party agencies, influencers or digital platforms does not, by itself, shield a doctor. Whichever agency you hire, the responsibility stays with you.

So your agency must refuse, not suggest, things like:

- Patient testimonial videos or shared patient reviews, even with consent.
- Before/after photos.
- Hoardings or posts saying "best doctor in Kanpur", "No.1" or "100% result".
- Buying or manipulating reviews, ratings, followers or likes.
- Discount offers or "free consultation" posts.
- Ads that solicit patients for an individual doctor.
- Pay-per-patient or pay-per-lead deals.
- AI-made patient faces, voices or stories.

## Seven questions to ask any agency

1. Have you worked with doctors or hospitals before? Can I see their public pages?

3. Will I approve every post before it goes live?
4. Will my Google profile, Instagram, website and domain stay in my name?
5. Is the fee fixed monthly, and what exactly is included?
6. Can you write scripts in simple Hindi or Hinglish for local patients?
7. If we stop working together, how will you hand over files and access?

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a digital marketing agency in Kanpur charge doctors?**
It depends on scope. Compare what is included (scripts, editing, posting, Google profile, website, reports) rather than only the monthly number. Our [cost guide](blog-digital-marketing-agency-cost-for-doctors.html) explains typical scope by budget.

**Can my agency put up hoardings or ads asking patients to visit?**
Promotion that solicits patients for an individual doctor is a problem under the NMC 2026 guidelines. Focus on patient education and correct, factual information so patients can find you.

**Do I need an agency based in Kanpur?**
Not necessarily. A healthcare-focused agency elsewhere can work well if it has a clear approval process and understands your local patients.

**Should my agency own my Instagram or Google profile?**
No. You should own every account. Give the agency manager or editor access only.

**Can a hospital share information about its new facilities?**
As reported by The South First, hospitals may share factual information about facilities, equipment and diagnostic services, as long as there are no claims of superiority or guaranteed outcomes.
