## Why online presence matters more for Patna doctors now

Big public hospitals in Patna are expanding. Medical Dialogues reported in July 2026 that Bihar's Health Secretary set a three-month deadline for a 500-bed teaching hospital at IGIMS Patna, and that IGIMS's proposed 1,200-bed hospital was then 59.24% complete. In the same month, it reported plans to expand AIIMS Patna by nearly 200 beds, with the state promising help with 24 acres of land.

For private clinics and smaller hospitals, the takeaway is simple: patients have many choices, and many will search on Google or Instagram before visiting. Being easy to find, with correct timings, location and clear information about what you treat, is now basic hygiene.

## Local search tips for Patna doctors

Even before hiring anyone, check these:

- Search your speciality plus your area (for example "child specialist Kankarbagh") and see if you appear.
- Make sure your clinic address on Google matches your website, letterhead and Practo-style listings exactly.
- Add the right primary category on Google (for example "Pediatrician", not just "Doctor").
- Keep holiday timings updated, especially around Chhath and other festivals when patients check before travelling.
- Reply to reviews politely and without sharing any patient details.

## What the NMC 2026 rules mean for Patna clinics

The NMC *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* (6 October 2026) apply to every doctor in Bihar, just as everywhere else. A Healthus.ai summary of the guidelines notes that:

- Outsourcing to an agency does not shift the doctor's or hospital's responsibility.
- Agency fees must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients.
- Doctors may not request or share patient testimonials or reviews for promotion, even with consent.
- Fake, paid or gated reviews and manipulating search rankings are prohibited.
- AI may not create or alter a patient's image, voice, testimonial or outcome.

So if a Patna agency pitches "No.1 doctor in Bihar" posters, happy-patient videos, review packages or pay-per-patient deals, say no. Read our [NMC new guidelines explainer](blog-nmc-new-guidelines-doctors-social-media.html) for the full picture.

## Questions to ask any agency in Patna

1. How many doctors or clinics do you work with now?
2. Who checks each post against the NMC rules, and will I approve every post?
3. Is your fee fixed monthly, or linked to patients or leads?
4. Will my Google profile, Instagram and website stay in my name?
5. Can you write scripts in Hindi or Hinglish?
6. Who shoots the videos: do you send a crew, or guide me to record on my phone?
7. If you are not based in Patna, how will we work together?

The last question matters because a good agency does not need to be in the same city. Much of the work (scripts, editing, profile updates, reporting) happens online, with calls and WhatsApp for approvals. What matters is responsiveness and a clear process. ## Frequently asked questions

**How do I choose a digital marketing agency for doctors in Patna?**
Check healthcare experience, NMC compliance process, post approval, fixed-fee pricing and account ownership. Location matters less than a clear process.

**Does the agency need an office in Patna?**
Not necessarily. Most of the work is done online. Ask how approvals and communication will work before you sign.

**Can a Patna agency run ads for my clinic?**
Ads must be factual and should not solicit patients for an individual doctor or make superlative claims. Ask the agency how it keeps ad content within the NMC rules.

**Should my content be in Hindi or English?**
Use the language your patients use. In Bihar, simple Hindi or Hinglish often works well for patient education.

**Is pay-per-patient marketing allowed?**
Summaries of the NMC 2026 guidelines say agency fees must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients. Choose a fixed fee instead.
