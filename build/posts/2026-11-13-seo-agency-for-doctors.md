## What SEO means for a clinic

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of making your website and Google Business Profile clear, correct and useful, so Google can show you to people who are searching for what you treat, in your area.

For a doctor, good SEO is mostly patient information done well:

- A website that loads fast on a phone and clearly lists your qualifications, registration details, specialities, timings and address.
- Simple pages that explain the conditions you treat and the services you offer, in plain language.
- A complete and correct Google Business Profile, with the right category, timings and location pin.
- The same name, address and phone number everywhere your clinic is listed.

None of this needs a "hack". It needs careful, regular work. If you want the basics first, read our guide on [SEO for doctors](blog-seo-for-doctors.html).

## The NMC 2026 rule every SEO agency must know

The National Medical Commission's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* came into force on 6 October 2026. As summarised by Healthus.ai, clause 8.1(xii) says there must be no manipulating of search rankings or algorithms to inflate professional standing. The same clause bars buying or manipulating followers, likes, reviews, ratings, comments, views or engagement.

The guidelines also treat websites and blogs as covered media. So the same rules on claims apply to your website text, not just to Instagram.

The summary also notes that publishing through a third party does not absolve the doctor or hospital, and that an intermediary cannot be used to do what you cannot do yourself. In simple words: if your SEO agency uses a banned trick, the responsibility is still yours.

This means an SEO agency for doctors in 2026 must avoid:

- Fake or paid Google reviews, or review "packages".
- Asking every patient for a 5-star review as part of an SEO plan.
- Pages that call you the "best", "No.1" or "top" doctor in the city.
- Paid link schemes and spammy directory listings meant to fool Google.
- Copying the same factual content again and again across many pages so that it turns into solicitation.

## What Google itself says about hiring an SEO

Google's Search Central guide "Do you need an SEO?" gives a useful list of questions to ask. These include asking for examples of previous work, whether they follow the Google Search Essentials, what results they expect and in what timeframe, and what experience they have in your industry and your city.

Google also gives clear warnings:

- "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google."
- Be careful if a company is secretive or won't clearly explain what they intend to do.
- Be wary of SEO firms that email you out of the blue.
- If an SEO offers an audit, give only read access to Search Console at first, not write access.

These warnings fit well with the NMC rules. An agency that promises "page 1 guaranteed" is breaking Google's advice. An agency that promises "guaranteed patients" is also a problem under the NMC guidelines.

## Red flags when choosing an SEO agency for doctors

| What they say | Why it is a problem |
|---|---|
| "Guaranteed No.1 on Google" | Google says no one can guarantee this |
| "We will get you 100 five-star reviews" | Buying or manipulating reviews is barred by NMC 8.1(xii) |
| "Pay us per patient or per lead" | Agency fees must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients |
| "We'll create the Google profile in our name" | You lose control if you stop working with them |
| "Just trust us, it's technical" | Google advises caution if a company won't explain its work |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can an SEO agency guarantee my clinic will rank first on Google?**
No. Google's own guide says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking. Treat any such promise as a red flag.

**Is SEO allowed for doctors under the NMC 2026 guidelines?**
Clear, factual website and profile work is fine. What is barred is manipulating search rankings or algorithms, and buying or manipulating reviews, ratings or engagement.

**Should I give my SEO agency full access to Google Search Console?**
Keep yourself as the owner. Google suggests giving only read access when an SEO first offers an audit. Give more access only once you trust their plan.

**Is SEO different from Google Business Profile work?**
They are linked. Your profile is part of local search, so a good SEO agency for doctors will work on both your website and your profile.

**How do I know if the SEO work is working?**
Ask for a monthly report from Google's own tools, such as Search Console and your Business Profile insights, showing searches, calls and direction requests.
