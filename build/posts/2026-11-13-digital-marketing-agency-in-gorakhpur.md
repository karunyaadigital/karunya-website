## The NMC 2026 rules your agency must follow

The National Medical Commission's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* came into force on 6 October 2026. As summarised by Healthus.ai, publishing through a third party does not absolve the doctor or hospital, and you may not use an intermediary to do what you cannot do yourself. So whichever agency you hire, the responsibility stays with you.

That is why your agency must refuse, not suggest, things like:

- Patient testimonial videos or "happy patient" stories, even with consent.
- Before/after photos.
- Posters saying "best doctor in Gorakhpur", "No.1" or "100% result".
- Buying or manipulating reviews, ratings, followers or likes.
- Discount offers or "free consultation" posts.
- Pay-per-patient or pay-per-lead deals. The summary notes agency fees must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients.
- AI-made patient faces, voices or stories.

## Local agency or an agency in another city?

Many Gorakhpur doctors ask whether the agency must be in the city. Here is an honest comparison:

| Point | Agency in Gorakhpur | Healthcare agency in another city |
|---|---|---|
| In-person meetings | Easy | Mostly video calls and WhatsApp |
| Local area knowledge | Usually strong | Needs your input on areas and patient language |
| Healthcare rules knowledge | Varies; many serve all industries | A healthcare-only agency checks NMC rules on every post |
| Shooting content | May offer a shoot team | You may record on your phone with their guidance |
| Approval process | Must be written either way | Must be written either way |

Neither is right for everyone. What matters more is whether the agency understands medical rules, gives you a written approval process and keeps every account in your name.

## Seven questions to ask any agency

1. Have you worked with doctors or hospitals before? Can I see their public pages?
2. Who checks each post and page against the NMC rules?
3. Will I approve every post before it goes live?
4. Will my Google profile, Instagram, website and domain stay in my name?
5. Is the fee fixed monthly, and what is included?
6. Can you write scripts in Hindi or Bhojpuri-friendly Hinglish for local patients?
7. If we stop working together, how will you hand over files and access?

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a digital marketing agency in Gorakhpur charge doctors?**
It depends on scope. Compare what is included (scripts, editing, posting, Google profile, website, reports) rather than only the monthly number. Our [cost guide](blog-digital-marketing-agency-cost-for-doctors.html) explains typical scope by budget.

**Can my agency run ads that ask patients to visit my clinic?**
Ads that solicit patients for an individual doctor are a problem under the NMC 2026 guidelines. Focus on patient education and correct, factual information so patients can find you.

**Do I need an agency based in Gorakhpur?**
Not necessarily. A healthcare-focused agency elsewhere can work well if it has a clear approval process and understands your local patients.

**Should my agency own my Instagram or Google profile?**
No. You should be the owner of every account. Give the agency manager or editor access only.

**Can I share patient reviews on my clinic's Instagram?**
The NMC summary says doctors may not request or share patient reviews for promotion, and patient consent does not change this. Read our [Google reviews guide](blog-google-reviews-for-doctors-nmc.html).
