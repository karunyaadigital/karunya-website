## How patients in Purvanchal search for a doctor

Search habits in district towns are a little different from big cities:

- **Hindi and Hinglish first.** Searches like "Ghazipur me haddi ka doctor", "Jaunpur dant ka doctor" or "Azamgarh bachon ka doctor" are common.
- **Google Maps and "near me".** Patients from villages often search "doctor near me" once they reach the town, then follow the map pin.
- **Landmark searches.** People describe clinics by chauraha, bus stand, railway station or a known temple, not by street name.
- **Varanasi comparisons.** Families often compare a district clinic with a Varanasi hospital before deciding where to go.
- **WhatsApp and phone calls.** Most patients want to call or message, not fill a form.

A good healthcare marketing company in eastern UP plans content and local pages around these habits.

## Local search checklist for district clinics

Whichever agency you hire, check these basics yourself:

1. **One verified Google profile**, owned by you or your clinic, with the agency only added as a manager.
2. **The same name, address and phone (NAP)** on your website, Google, Facebook, Instagram and listing sites.
3. **Exact pin code and a landmark** in the address, such as "near Kachahri" or "opposite roadways bus stand".
4. **Correct category**, for example "Orthopedic surgeon" or "Dentist", not just "Clinic".
5. **Updated timings**, including market days, Sundays and festivals like Chhath.
6. **Real photos** of the signboard, reception and waiting area, with no patients visible.
7. **Hindi and English** on key pages, because many patients search in Hindi.

Google's newer "Ask Maps" feature, described by Search Engine Journal in June 2026, answers detailed questions using profile details such as hours, reviews and attributes. Complete, accurate profile data matters more than ever.

## What the NMC 2026 rules mean for Purvanchal doctors

The NMC's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* (6 October 2026) apply in every town, not just metros. Using an agency does not move responsibility away from the doctor. In short:

- **Allowed:** factual information (name, qualifications, registration number, timings, services), patient education, health awareness and correct Google details.
- **Not allowed:** patient testimonials, before/after photos, "best", "No. 1" or "guaranteed" claims, discount or free-consultation offers, buying or soliciting reviews, pay-per-patient deals with agencies, and AI-made patient stories.
- **Dentists:** the National Dental Commission's advisory of 14 October 2026 adds similar limits for dental clinics.

Enforcement is real. Medical Dialogues reported that the NMC has set penalties for advertising violations that include suspension of up to 12 months. And UNI reported on 16 November 2026 that the Jharkhand State Medical Council gave doctors and nursing homes one week to remove promotional ads carrying doctors' photographs, warning of fines and registration suspension. Hoardings with large doctor photos are still common on Purvanchal highways, so this is worth checking now.

## What drives the cost

Agency fees in Purvanchal depend on a few things:

- **Scope:** Google profile only, or Google plus Instagram, reels and website.
- **Speciality:** a dental or IVF clinic needs different content from a general physician.
- **Number of doctors and branches** at a nursing home or hospital.
- **Website needs:** a new site or fixes to an old one.
- **Language:** bilingual content takes more time than English-only.

Ask for a fixed monthly fee with a written scope. Avoid fees linked to the number of patients or leads; the NMC guidelines bar them. Our [digital marketing agency cost guide for doctors](blog-digital-marketing-agency-cost-for-doctors.html) explains this in more detail.
