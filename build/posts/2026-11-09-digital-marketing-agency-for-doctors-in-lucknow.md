## The 2026 rules every Lucknow agency must follow

The NMC's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* (6 October 2026) apply to doctors everywhere in India, including Lucknow. According to a summary by Healthus.ai:

- Doctors remain responsible for content they "authorised, commissioned, sponsored or knowingly permitted", even when an agency made it.
- Agency fees must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients.
- Patient testimonials, before/after photos and patient success stories are not allowed. Patient consent does not make a testimonial acceptable.
- Buying or manipulating reviews, ratings or engagement is prohibited.
- AI may not create patient images, voices or outcomes, and permitted AI promotional content must be labelled.

So the agency you hire is writing in your name. Our [NMC new guidelines explainer](blog-nmc-new-guidelines-doctors-social-media.html) covers the rules in more detail.

## How to compare agencies in Lucknow

| Option | Good fit when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| General local agency | It has a medical team and knows the NMC rules | Restaurant-style offers and "best" claims |
| Freelancer | You need one skill, such as editing | No compliance check, no backup |
| Healthcare-focused agency (local or remote) | You want Google, website and content handled together | Check the approval process and contract terms |

An agency does not have to sit in your city to do local work. Google profile, website and content work is done online. What matters is that they understand your locality, your patients' language and the rules. If you prefer someone who can visit, ask about that directly.

## Red flags in a proposal

- Testimonial videos or "patient stories"
- Before/after photos
- "Best doctor in Lucknow", "No.1" or "guaranteed results" in your posts
- Buying followers, views or reviews, or asking patients for reviews in return for anything
- Discount or free-consultation offers
- Payment per patient or per lead

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. Who checks every post against the NMC rules?
2. Will I approve each post before it goes live?
3. What exactly is delivered each month, in numbers?
4. Will my Google profile, website and Instagram stay in my name?
5. What will the monthly report show that I can verify myself?
