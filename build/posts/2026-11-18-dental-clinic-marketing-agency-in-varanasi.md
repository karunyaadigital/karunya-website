## Why Varanasi dentists need a clear online presence

Varanasi is not short of dentists. The city has the Faculty of Dental Sciences at Banaras Hindu University, a constituent college listed by Careers360 as established in 2005 with 63 BDS seats, and many private clinics across the city. Directory sites such as WhatClinic list Varanasi dentists for services from root canals to implants.

With so many options, patients compare before they call. Most of them compare on their phones: Google reviews, timings, photos of the clinic and a few posts that show the dentist explaining things clearly. A focused online presence helps patients find you and trust the information, without any of the hype the rules now ban.

## The rules every Varanasi dental agency must follow

**NDC advisory (14 October 2026).** As reported by Medical Dialogues, the advisory warns dentists against filming or livestreaming consultations and procedures, posting patient reviews and before-and-after photos, misleading treatment claims, inaccurate health information, excessive self-promotion and oversized signboards. Violations can lead to a show-cause notice, suspension or removal from the register.

**Scope of practice (reported 13 November 2026).** Telangana Today reported an NDC Ethics and Dental Registration Board advisory explaining that BDS graduates handle routine work such as fillings, scaling, simple extractions and basic root canals, while complex work such as advanced implants and jaw surgery is for MDS specialists. For marketing, this means your posts should never present you as offering procedures outside your training. A careful agency checks this before writing about implants or surgery.

The NMC's 2026 guidelines follow the same spirit for doctors: no testimonials, no "best" or "100% painless" claims, no buying reviews and no discount offers. Our [dental marketing agency India guide](blog-dental-marketing-agency-india.html) covers the NDC advisory in more detail.

## Local search tips for Varanasi dental clinics

- **Name your area clearly.** Patients search by locality, so your Google profile and website should state the exact address and nearby landmark.
- **Keep timings honest.** If you take emergency calls, say when. If you are closed on a day, mark it.
- **Use real clinic photos.** The chair, the sterilisation area, the waiting room, all without patients.
- **Write in the language patients use.** Many patients in eastern UP are more comfortable in Hindi, so Hindi or Hinglish captions help.
- **Reply to every review politely.** Thank people, never reveal treatment details, and never offer anything in return for reviews.

## Red flags in a dental agency's proposal

Say no if an agency offers:

1. Before/after smile galleries or "smile transformation" Reels.
2. Live videos of procedures in your chair.
3. "Rs X off on teeth whitening" or free check-up camp ads.
4. Guaranteed rankings or "best dentist in Varanasi" posts.
5. Pay per lead or per patient fees.
6. Accounts created in the agency's name instead of yours.

## Questions to ask before hiring

- Have you worked with dentists since the NDC advisory? Can I see their pages?
- Who checks posts against NDC rules, and will I approve every post?
- How do you make sure posts match my BDS or MDS scope?
- Do you send a camera crew, or guide me to record on my phone?
- Will the Google profile, Instagram and website stay in my name?
- Is the fee a fixed monthly amount?

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a dentist in Varanasi still post on Instagram after the NDC advisory?**
Yes. Education posts about oral health, clinic timings and your qualifications are fine. Avoid filming procedures, before/after photos, patient reviews and "best" claims.

**Can I show my clinic and equipment in posts?**
Yes, as factual information, as long as no patient is visible and you don't make claims about results.

**Can I post about dental implants if I am a BDS dentist?**
General education about what implants are is different from offering advanced implant surgery. Since the NDC has said complex implants are MDS-level work, keep any post about your own services within your training.

**How long before a Google profile brings calls?**
It depends on your area, competition and how complete the profile is. A good agency fixes the basics first and reports calls and direction requests every month, without promising a number.
