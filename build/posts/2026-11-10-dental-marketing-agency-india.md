Search for a **dental marketing agency in India** and you will see agency pages, directory listings and "top 10" lists. Many of the sample posts on those pages still show smile makeovers, patient videos and "best dentist" banners. Since October 2026, a lot of that is a problem for dentists.

This guide is for dentists and dental clinic owners who are deciding which agency to hire. It explains what a dental marketing agency should do, what the National Dental Commission's advisory says, how to compare agencies and the questions to ask before you sign.

## Why dentists need to be extra careful now

On 14 October 2026, the Ethics and Dental Registration Board (EDRB) of the National Dental Commission issued an advisory to all registered dental practitioners, as reported by Medical Dialogues. It was based on Chapter VI (Unethical Acts) of the Revised Dentists (Code of Ethics) Regulations, 2014.

The advisory flagged these practices:

- Recording or broadcasting consultations and dental procedures
- Publishing patient testimonials and before-and-after photos
- Misleading treatment claims
- Circulating inaccurate health information
- Self-promotional publicity
- Disproportionately large signboards

According to the report, an established violation may lead to a show-cause notice, suspension, or removal from the State and National Registers of Dentists.

This matters for agency hiring because many common dental marketing ideas, such as smile transformation Reels, "watch this root canal" videos and patient review videos, fall squarely in that list. The agency you hire posts in your name, but you carry the risk.

## What a dental marketing agency should do now

| Work | What it includes | Why it matters for a dental clinic |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Correct dental category, timings, map pin, clinic photos, review replies | Most patients find a dentist on Google Maps |
| Website | Dentist profile, qualifications, treatments explained, location and timings | Patients check who will treat them before they call |
| Local SEO | Pages for treatments and your area | Visibility for "dentist + area" searches |
| Instagram and Reels | Education scripts, shoot guidance, editing, posting | Explaining treatments in simple words builds trust |
| Compliance check | Every post checked against NDC rules before you approve | Keeps your registration safe |

Good educational topics are still open: how to brush and floss properly, signs that you need a check-up, what a root canal is and when it is needed, how to care for braces, and what to do in a dental emergency. These explain care without showing patients or promising results. For more topic ideas, see our [dental clinic marketing ideas](blog-dental-clinic-marketing-ideas.html).

## What to avoid in a dental agency's proposal

| If the proposal says... | Why it's a problem |
|---|---|
| "Smile makeover before/after posts" | Before-and-after photos are flagged by the advisory |
| "Patient testimonial videos" | Testimonials are flagged by the advisory |
| "Film your procedures for Reels" | Recording or broadcasting procedures is flagged |
| "Best dentist in [city]" | Self-promotional and unverifiable |
| "Guaranteed pain-free" or "100% results" | Misleading treatment claims |
| "Free check-up camp offer" or discounts | Self-promotional inducement; avoid |
| "We'll get you 100 Google reviews" | Buying or soliciting reviews is risky and can be fake |
| "Pay us per patient" | Payment tied to patients is a red flag across healthcare |

If an agency's sample work for other dentists still looks like this, ask them how they have changed their process since October.

## Specialist dental agency or general healthcare agency?

| Option | Good fit when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Dental-only agency | You want someone who knows dental treatments well | Check that samples follow the 2026 advisory |
| Healthcare agency | It has dental clients and a compliance check for NDC rules | Make sure they don't apply only NMC rules |
| General local agency | It has a medical team and understands the rules | Restaurant-style offers and "best" claims |
| Freelancer | You need one skill, like editing | No compliance check, no backup |

The label matters less than the process. Ask who checks posts against the NDC advisory and whether you approve each post before it goes live.

## Questions to ask any dental marketing agency

1. Have you read the NDC advisory of 14 October 2026? How did it change your work?
2. Who checks each post, and do I approve it before it goes live?
3. What exactly will you deliver each month, in numbers?
4. Will my Google profile, Instagram and website stay in my name?
5. How are you paid: fixed fee, project fee, or something linked to patients?
6. What will the monthly report show, and can I check it myself?

## Frequently asked questions

**Can dentists post before-and-after photos on Instagram?**
The NDC advisory of 14 October 2026 flags publishing before-and-after photos as an unethical practice. Treat it as off-limits.

**Can a dental agency record my procedures for Reels?**
The advisory flags recording or broadcasting consultations and procedures. Explain treatments yourself on camera instead, without patients.

**Do the NMC guidelines apply to dentists?**
Dentists are registered with the dental councils and follow the Dentists (Code of Ethics) Regulations and NDC advisories. The NDC advisory is the main reference for dental clinics.
