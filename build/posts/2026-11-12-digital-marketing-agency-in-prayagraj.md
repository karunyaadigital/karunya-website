## Why being found online matters for Prayagraj doctors

The city's healthcare map is growing. Medical Dialogues reported in October 2026 that Park Hospital (Park Medi World) marked the groundbreaking of a 550-bed multi-super-speciality hospital in Prayagraj, built at about Rs 200 crore under a public-private partnership with the Prayagraj Municipal Corporation, with completion targeted within two years. Earlier, in March 2026, PTI reported (via ThePrint) an MoU to set up AI-based super speciality ICU facilities at Tej Bahadur Sapru (Bailey) Hospital and Motilal Nehru Divisional (Colvin) Hospital, which the Deputy Chief Minister said could benefit about 1.4 crore people in the Prayagraj division.

For a private clinic or smaller hospital, this means more choice for patients. Many of them will search on Google or Instagram before they travel. Correct timings, a clear location and simple information about what you treat are now basic needs, not extras.

## Local search tips before you hire anyone

You can check a few things yourself today:

- Search your speciality plus your area (for example "skin doctor Civil Lines") and see whether you appear.
- Make sure the clinic name and address on Google exactly match your website and letterhead.
- Pick the right primary category on Google, such as "Orthopedic surgeon", not just "Doctor".
- Keep holiday and special timings updated, especially during big melas and festivals when roads and patient travel change.
- Reply to reviews politely without sharing any patient details.

## The NMC 2026 rules every Prayagraj clinic must follow

The NMC *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* came into force on 6 October 2026 and apply to every doctor in Uttar Pradesh. As The South First reported, the rules also cover promotion done through an agency, influencer or other intermediary, and using one does not shield the doctor if they authorised or knowingly permitted the content. Doctors must also show their name, qualifications and registration number on electronic media posts.

So refuse any agency, local or remote, that suggests:

- Patient testimonial videos or before/after photos.
- "Best doctor in Prayagraj", "No.1" or "100% result" claims.
- Buying reviews or asking every patient for 5 stars.
- Discount or free-consultation offers.
- Pay-per-patient or pay-per-lead deals.
- AI-made patient faces, voices or stories.

Our [NMC new guidelines explainer](blog-nmc-new-guidelines-doctors-social-media.html) covers the full list.

## Questions to ask any agency

1. How many doctors or clinics do you work with now?
2. Who checks each post against the NMC rules, and do I approve every post?
3. Is the fee a fixed monthly amount? What is included?
4. Will my Google profile, Instagram, domain and website stay in my name?
5. Can you write scripts in Hindi or Hinglish for my patients?
6. Who shoots the videos: a crew, or guidance for me to record on my phone?
7. If you are not based in Prayagraj, how will approvals and calls work?

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I choose a digital marketing agency in Prayagraj for my clinic?**
Check healthcare experience, the NMC checking process, your approval on every post, a fixed fee and that all accounts stay in your name.

**Does the agency need an office in Prayagraj?**
Not necessarily. Most of the work is done online. What matters is a clear approval process and quick replies.

**Can an agency run Google ads for my clinic?**
Ads must stay factual, avoid superlative claims and must not solicit patients for an individual doctor. Ask how the agency keeps ad content within the NMC rules.

**Should my posts be in Hindi or English?**
Use the language your patients speak. In Prayagraj, simple Hindi or Hinglish often works well for patient education.

**Is pay-per-patient marketing allowed?**
No. Summaries of the NMC 2026 guidelines say agency payment must not be linked to procuring or referring individual patients.
